Octopus Agent

A personal automation assistant for your Google services

What this app does

Octopus Agent is a personal automation assistant that connects to your Google account to keep your services working together. It manages the secure authentication that lets your automation tools read and organize your email, documents, and calendar on your behalf.

  • Keeps your Google services connected with secure, automatic authentication
  • Enables your personal automation workflows across Gmail, Drive, and Calendar
  • Operates only on your own account — no third-party users, no data sharing
